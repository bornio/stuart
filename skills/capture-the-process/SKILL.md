# Capture the process

The person does most of the job without thinking about it. The agent needs that knowledge written down. Get it in three passes: brain dump, play back, then real examples.

## 1. Brain dump

Ask them to talk, by voice if they can, without organizing — or point you at 2–3 past docs, threads, or sent emails to read (without acting) so you can draft the steps and cases for them to correct. Tell them a messy dump is fine. Ask them to cover:

- the steps they take
- what they watch out for
- the apps and tools they use
- where the information comes from: email, chat, a ticket, a website, a phone call, a file on their computer
- how they check the work was done right
- where they hear how it went: reviews, customers, coworkers

## 2. Play back

Restate the process in 5 to 8 plain steps. Mark each guess you made. Ask what is wrong or missing. Repeat until they say it is right. Planning only: leave the work itself for the agent.

## 3. Judgment calls

Ask for three real cases, including one that went wrong or needed a different call (for a brand-new job with no past cases, walk through 2 live examples together to set the bar). Walk each case through the played-back steps out loud. Then ask one question at a time from this list, skipping any already answered:

- Which source wins when two disagree? Example: the email says 3pm and the booking app says 4pm.
- What does done mean? Sent is not done. Saved, confirmed, and recorded is done.
- What changes the plan? Which jobs take longer, who can do what, when to leave extra time.
- What is still open, and how does the agent track it so a retry does not do the work twice?
- When should the agent stop and hand the case back to the person instead of deciding?
- What must the agent never do? Include domain defaults when they fit:
  - For external drafts or numbers: cite the source for every figure or claim, flag unsourced claims instead of guessing, and note when an unreadable channel (texts, WhatsApp) may mean missed recent contact.
  - For people or money data (candidates, employees, comp, contracts): keep IDs and links only in memory (never raw contents), use invented look-alike saved cases, and never rank or reject people or infer protected traits.

Write each answer as a rule in the plan. Save the cases in the plan too, each with minimal de-identified facts and the outcome that was right. For public writing or outreach, save 2 full-text examples they loved, 1 they would never send, and banned words (with private recipient details scrubbed). Then ask: "What have I not asked about yet?"

## 4. Better way

Check the process against the goal. Flag any step that does not serve the goal, any newly surfaced step an existing app handles natively, and any step an agent could do differently (connector, small script, or — only where logins and site rules allow — browser). Offer the change. Keep their way if they prefer it.

Done when the person confirms the steps and the rules, and the saved cases are in the plan.
